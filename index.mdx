Design and manufacture real, useful printed circuit boards from scratch using KiCad 9. Each lesson produces one complete board for embedded systems, starting from a simple through-hole ATmega328P breakout and progressing to multi-layer STM32 and ESP32 boards with USB-C, WiFi, battery management, and motor control. Every board includes minimal firmware to verify it works. #KiCad #PCBDesign #EmbeddedSystems

## Course Structure

Each lesson follows a consistent cycle:

<Steps>
1. **The Board**
   What we are building, why it is useful, and the design choices behind it.

2. **Schematic Capture**
   Component selection, wiring, power distribution, ERC. Full walkthrough in KiCad 9.

3. **PCB Layout**
   Component placement strategy, routing, copper pours, design rules, DRC.

4. **Manufacturing Output**
   Gerbers, drill files, BOM, and the manufacturing method for that lesson (etching, CNC milling, or fab house).

5. **Build and Test**
   Soldering guidance, first power-on checklist, and minimal firmware to confirm the board works.
</Steps>

  <Card title="Lesson 1: ATmega328P Breakout Board" icon="star">
    [ATmega328P Through-Hole Breakout](./atmega328p-breakout-board-through-hole). Build a minimal Arduino-compatible board with ISP header, power LED, and pin headers. Learn KiCad 9 from scratch: project setup, schematic capture, PCB layout, and home etching. First firmware: blink an LED via ISP.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 2: ATmega328P Sensor Shield" icon="document">
    [ATmega328P Sensor Shield with SMD](./atmega328p-sensor-shield-smd-components). Upgrade to SMD with an ATmega328P TQFP-32 board carrying I2C temperature/humidity and SPI light sensors. Introduction to SMD footprints, double-sided layout, and CNC milling for PCB fabrication.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 3: STM32 USB Development Board" icon="document">
    [STM32 USB Development Board](./stm32-usb-development-board). Design a Blue Pill style board with STM32F103, USB, crystal oscillator, SWD debug header, and 3.3V regulation. First professional fab house order: generate Gerbers, BOM, and pick-and-place files for JLCPCB.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 4: STM32 USB-C Four-Layer Board" icon="document">
    [STM32 USB-C Four-Layer PCB](./stm32-usb-c-four-layer-pcb). Step up to a Black Pill style STM32F411 board with USB-C, LDO power regulation, and a 4-layer stackup. Learn power/ground plane design, via stitching, and impedance-aware routing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 5: ESP32 WiFi/Bluetooth DevKit" icon="document">
    [ESP32 WiFi Bluetooth DevKit](./esp32-wifi-bluetooth-devkit). Integrate an ESP32-WROOM module with USB-UART bridge, antenna keepout zone, auto-reset circuit, and impedance-matched RF trace. Layout considerations for wireless performance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 6: ESP32 Battery IoT Sensor Node" icon="document">
    [ESP32 Battery IoT Sensor Node](./esp32-battery-iot-sensor-node). Design a battery-powered ESP32-C3 board with TP4056 charge IC, solar input, BME280 environmental sensor, and deep sleep circuitry. Power-aware layout for long battery life.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 7: RP2040 USB-C Development Board" icon="document">
    [RP2040 USB-C Development Board](./rp2040-usb-c-development-board). Build a board around the RP2040 with external flash, native USB-C, PIO headers, and RGB LED. QFN soldering considerations, high-speed routing, and 4-layer design.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 8: Motor Driver & Sensor Integration" icon="document">
    [Motor Driver Sensor Integration Board](./motor-driver-sensor-integration-board). Combine a DRV8833 H-bridge motor driver, MPU6050 IMU, current sensing, and screw terminals on one board. Mixed power and signal routing, thermal management, and protection circuits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lesson 9: Code-Based PCB Design" icon="document">
    [Code-Based PCB Design with KiCad Scripting](./code-based-pcb-design-kicad-scripting). Recreate Lesson 1's board using Python and KiCad's scripting API. Introduction to SKiDL for programmatic netlists, parametric footprints, and automated DRC. A bridge to the future of code-driven electronics design.
  </Card>

## Skill Progression

| Skill | Lessons 1-2 | Lessons 3-4 | Lessons 5-6 | Lessons 7-8 | Lesson 9 |
|-------|------------|------------|------------|------------|----------|
| **Components** | Through-hole, basic SMD | Fine-pitch SMD, USB | RF modules, battery ICs | QFN, motor drivers | Programmatic |
| **Layers** | 1-2 layer | 2-4 layer | 2 layer (RF-aware) | 4 layer | Any |
| **Routing** | Manual, wide traces | Differential pairs intro | Impedance matching | High-speed, power | Automated |
| **Manufacturing** | Home etching, CNC mill | Fab house (JLCPCB) | Fab house | Fab house | Scripted output |
| **Firmware** | AVR ISP blink | USB CDC serial | WiFi scan, deep sleep | USB-C, PID control | None |

## Prerequisites

<Badge text="No prior KiCad experience needed" variant="tip" />

**You should be comfortable with:**
- Basic electronics (voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's law)
- Reading a simple schematic (component symbols, connections)
- Using a computer confidently (file management, installing software)

**Helpful but not required:**
- Soldering experience (through-hole or SMD)
- Arduino or embedded programming basics
- Our [Code-Based Mechanical Design](/education/code-based-mechanical-design/) course (for enclosure design)

## Getting Started

<Steps>
1. **Install KiCad 9** from [kicad.org](https://www.kicad.org/download/)
   KiCad runs on Linux, macOS, and Windows. The installer includes all libraries.

2. **Start with Lesson 1**, which walks through KiCad 9 setup and produces your first complete PCB.

3. **Work through lessons sequentially.** Each builds on skills and patterns from earlier lessons.

4. **Build the boards.** The course is designed around manufacturing. Etching Lesson 1 at home or ordering Lesson 3 from JLCPCB is part of the learning.

5. **Flash the firmware.** Each lesson includes minimal code to verify your board works.
</Steps>

:::tip[Have a soldering iron?]
Lessons 1 and 2 produce boards you can etch or mill at home and solder yourself. From Lesson 3 onward, you can order professionally manufactured boards from JLCPCB or PCBWay for a few dollars, often with free shipping on your first order.
:::
